POLYMETRIC SEQUENCER

Every channel runs its own 32-step grid with an independent loop length - layer polymeters and patterns that drift in and out of phase. Per-step velocity, accent, modulation, global swing, and sample-accurate timing.

EIGHT LIVE CHANNELS

GUR opens with all eight channels ready. Any slot can host any engine, so the rack fits the set you are playing.

GLOBAL OR CHANNEL PATTERNS

The pattern row can target the whole live set or one selected channel. Switch CH1-CH8 into focus, load or save a single channel pattern, then return to global mode for full-pattern changes.

256-BAR PIANO ROLL

Write long-form melodic parts inside the live sequencer - up to 256 bars per channel, with drawn note lengths, per-beat velocity, accent/open states, copied automation sections, and real-time read/write recording.

PERCUSSION SYNTH / SAMPLER

A flexible Percussion engine sits alongside Kick, Snare, Hats, Bass, Lead and the classics. Load it into any live channel slot.

FOUR FLEXIBLE DECKS

Four decks, each freely assignable - load a sequencer pattern or an audio track onto any of them. The full CDJ workflow, in software.

DJ LIBRARY & IMPORT

A built-in collection with playlists. Import straight from Traktor, Rekordbox and VirtualDJ - BPM, beatgrids, keys and hot cues come across - or read a live Rekordbox database directly.

HARMONIC KEY MIXING

Automatic musical / Camelot key per deck, with real transposition. Mix in key across sequencer and track decks, and shift any deck to match.

CDJ-GRADE LOOPS & CUES

Beat loops, manual loops and slip mode, plus 8 saved loops and 8 hot cues per deck - phase-locked to the sequencer with continuous beat-sync, and a direct route into LiveFX.

KEY-LOCK TIME-STRETCH

High-quality stretching keeps pitch locked while you ride the tempo - or unlock it for full vinyl-style pitch bends.

STEM SEPARATION

Offline 4-stem separation per track - vocals, drums, bass, other. Mute, solo, set the level of each stem and route any stem into LiveFX - all straight from the deck.

STUDIO-QUALITY EXPORT

Render your set to WAV, AIFF, FLAC, MP3 or AAC - at the right tempo and key, with full metadata. A faithful recording of exactly what you heard.

INSERT FX + LIVEFX

Two effect systems with two jobs: inserts shape each channel, LiveFX is what you play.

INSERT FX - 13 NATIVE EFFECTS, 8 SLOTS PER CHANNEL

The PRO MIXER gives every channel 8 insert slots, EQ, high-pass and low-pass filters, trim and pan. The suite covers compressor, limiter, flanger, phaser, crusher, trance gate, chorus, delay, ping-pong, echo, spring, plate and room. Each effect has its own editor with factory and user presets, A/B and undo. Inserts save with your patterns, reorder by drag and drop, and can be automated from the piano roll.

LIVEFX - THROW EFFECTS YOU PLAY

Hold a pad to throw flanger, phaser, crusher, trance gate, roll, beat-loop, glitch, delay, reverb, ping-pong or echo, plus filter sweeps. LiveFX stays locked to the beat and engages on the grid. A performance panel adds an XY pad, macro controls and HOLD. Sequencer channels, track decks and individual stems can all feed it, and it maps straight to your controller.

MADE TO BE PLAYED BY HAND

GUR maps by action, not by device type, so DJ gear, keyboards, pads and fader banks can all drive it.

THE FLAGSHIPS

GUR was developed hands-on with the flagship controllers of Native Instruments, Pioneer DJ, Akai and Novation, including jog wheels, touch strips, pad grids and LED feedback.

EVERY MAJOR BRAND

The built-in list has 177 devices in 8 categories, from Denon DJ, Numark, Rane, Reloop, Hercules, Allen & Heath, Arturia, Roland, Korg, Behringer, Elektron and more. A few flagships ship with ready-made mappings; the rest are mapped with MIDI Learn. Not on the list? Add your own.

SEVERAL AT ONCE

Each connected controller keeps its own mapping, so several can run together without colliding. Each device has its own profile, and GUR reconnects when you plug in.

QUESTIONS

What is GUR?

GUR is a DJ Live Sequencer Workstation by 21st Century Artisan: a desktop instrument for Windows and macOS that combines a live sequencer, 12 instrument engines, four DJ decks and stem separation.

Does GUR support VST plugins?

GUR itself runs as a VST3 inside your DAW. It cannot load third-party plugins today. Hosting third-party VST3 effects is on the roadmap for release by January 1, 2027, as a free update.

Is GUR a closed ecosystem?

Today GUR includes its own instruments and effects and does not host third-party plugins. It does run in any VST3 DAW, imports Traktor, Rekordbox and VirtualDJ libraries, exports standard audio files and works with MIDI controllers from any brand. The roadmap above lists what opens it further.

Is GUR a DAW?

Not yet. GUR is a live performance instrument today. Its DAW tier, GUR PRO, is in development and on the roadmap for release by January 1, 2027. It adds a post-production timeline and audio channels and is priced as DAW software, with 50% off for GUR DJ owners.

Which MIDI controllers work with GUR?

GUR maps by action, so any MIDI controller can be mapped with MIDI Learn. It was developed with the flagships of Native Instruments, Pioneer DJ, Akai and Novation, and its list has 177 devices across the major brands.

How much does GUR cost?

GUR DJ is $69.99 and GUR LITE is $39.99 at launch pricing, rising to $129.99 and $69.99 from January 1. Both are one-time purchases with no subscription, all v1.x updates free and a 30-day full-featured trial. GUR PRO, the upcoming DAW tier, will be priced separately, with 50% off for GUR DJ owners.
